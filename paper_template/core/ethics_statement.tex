\section*{Ethics Statement}

This research uses only publicly available, de-identified benchmark datasets (DDXPlus, MedMCQA, and MedQA-USMLE) and does not involve any human participants, identifiable patient data, or clinical intervention. The proposed framework is intended as a decision-support and research artefact and is not deployed for autonomous clinical decision-making. Model outputs are reported with calibrated uncertainty and explicit source attribution to support, rather than replace, qualified clinical judgement.
